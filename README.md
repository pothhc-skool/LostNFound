Proposed Project Idea
- Lost and Found
  A lost and found application, which is solely focused on individual's who frequent FSC's campus, that provides a tool for matching lost and found requests made by people throughout FSC's campus.
  For example, if one individual creates a post saying "I have found a 'black iPhone 15 with a red case and white dog as the background' on the basketball court in Nold Hall!" while another post that either existed prior to the initial post or was created after states,
  "I have lost my 'black iPhone 15, that has a red case and a white dog as the background' in Nold hall on the basketball court!" This would lead to an A.I developed system that tracks these two posts and connects them based on related keywords such as the location, description, time or characteristics.
